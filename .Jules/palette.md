## 2025-05-18 - Audio Player Icon-Only Buttons and Sliders Missing ARIA Labels
**Learning:** Audio player components with icon-only control buttons (Play, Pause, Skip Back, Skip Forward) and range sliders (track position, volume) lack explicit `aria-label` attributes, rendering them inaccessible to screen readers.
**Action:** Always provide explicit, dynamic `aria-label`s on icon-only buttons and sliders in media controls.
