## 2025-05-18 - Audio Player Icon Buttons & Sliders Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only media player controls (Play/Pause, Seek, Skip, Volume) require explicit `aria-label` attributes and `aria-hidden="true"` on decorative inner icons so screen reader users can navigate and control audio playback seamlessly.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive `aria-label`s on icon-only buttons and sliders in media controls.
