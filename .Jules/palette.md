## 2025-02-18 - Audio Controls Accessible ARIA Labels
**Learning:** Audio player icon-only buttons (`SkipBack`, `SkipForward`, `Play`/`Pause`) and Radix UI sliders (`Seek`, `Volume`) require explicit `aria-label` attributes for screen readers to announce control actions and slider purpose accurately.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive dynamic/static `aria-label` attributes on icon-only control buttons and range sliders in custom media players.
